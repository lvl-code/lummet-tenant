Wagering Requirements Explained (The Math)

A bonus is never just free money. Casinos protect themselves using Wagering Requirements (also known as Playthrough Requirements). This metric dictates the total volume of bets you must place before the bonus funds convert into real, withdrawable cash.

Example Calculation:
You claim a 100% match bonus on a €100 deposit. You now have €100 Cash + €100 Bonus. The terms state a 35x wagering requirement on the Bonus amount only.

  • Bonus Amount = €100
  • Multiplier = 35x
  • Total Playthrough Required = €100 * 35 = €3,500

This means you must place €3,500 worth of bets (wins and losses combined) on eligible games.

Beware the "Deposit + Bonus" Trap:
Some aggressive casinos state "30x Wagering on Deposit + Bonus". Using the same €100 deposit example, the math changes drastically: (€100 + €100) * 30 = €6,000 required playthrough. Always read the fine print to identify which mathematical model the casino is using.
