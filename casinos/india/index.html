FEMA Compliance & Playing in ₹ INR

One of the critical factors for Indian players is currency. Under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), exchanging INR to foreign currencies for the explicit purpose of gambling can raise banking red flags. To solve this, the top-tier offshore casinos allow you to deposit, play, and withdraw directly in Indian Rupees (₹). This completely bypasses expensive conversion fees and ensures your banking transactions appear standard.

Are online casinos strictly legal in India?

Gambling in India is a state-level subject. While states like Goa and Sikkim have legalized certain forms of gambling, and states like Andhra Pradesh have banned online gaming, there is no federal law prosecuting players for using offshore casino sites. As long as the casino is headquartered outside India and offers INR as a currency, it falls into a safe gray area utilized by millions.

Can I play native Indian games for real money?

Yes. Leading software providers like Evolution Gaming and Ezugi have created dedicated studios for the Indian market. You can play Real Money Teen Patti, Andar Bahar, Lucky 7, and Hindi Roulette with professional, native-speaking live dealers 24/7.

Do I have to pay taxes on my casino winnings?

In India, net winnings from online games are technically subject to a 30% Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under Section 115BBJ of the Income Tax Act. However, offshore, internationally licensed crypto casinos often leave tax reporting to the individual player rather than automatically deducting it at the source.
