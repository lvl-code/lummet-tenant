How do I know if a UK casino is safe?

Always check the footer of the casino website for the UKGC logo and a valid license number. You can verify this number directly on the UK Gambling Commission website. Every brand we recommend on Level.Casino has undergone thorough auditing to ensure they meet these strict safety requirements.

Are my winnings taxed in the UK?

No. In the United Kingdom, gambling winnings are currently tax-free for players. The government taxes the casino operators directly on their profits, meaning the amount you withdraw from your balance is exactly the amount you keep.

What is the KYC process like in the UK?

Due to UK law, casinos must verify your identity (Know Your Customer) before you can play or withdraw. This usually involves an automated check against the electoral roll. If that fails, you'll need to provide a photo of your ID and a utility bill. Many 'Fast Track' casinos now complete this process instantly during registration.
