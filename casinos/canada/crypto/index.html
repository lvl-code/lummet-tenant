Section 8: What Is Provably Fair Gaming?

One of the biggest advantages of blockchain gambling is provably fair gaming. Traditional online casinos rely on external testing labs to verify their Random Number Generators (RNG). In contrast, provably fair platforms use open-source cryptographic verification tools that let players check the fairness of every spin or game outcome manually.

The system verifies game outcomes using three core elements:

1. Server Seed A unique cryptographic key generated by the casino's system before the round begins.
2. Client Seed A variable key supplied by your web browser, giving you direct control over the outcome data.
3. Nonce Tracking A counter that increases with every bet you place, creating a clear history of your game outcomes.

Before you place a bet, the casino provides an encrypted hash of the Server Seed. Once the round ends, the unhashed seed is revealed. You can then plug these values into an independent tool to verify that the game outcome was random and free from manipulation.

Section 10: Taxes and Crypto Gambling in Canada

Understanding tax rules is essential when playing with digital assets. In Canada, windfall winnings from casual hobby gambling are generally non-taxable. However, because cryptocurrencies are treated as commodities by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), using digital tokens can create complex tax scenarios.

General Considerations & Record Keeping

If you trade digital assets frequently or are classified as a professional gambler, your returns may be subject to income or capital gains tax. Additionally, swapping tokens or converting crypto winnings back into Canadian Dollars (CAD) can trigger taxable events based on price changes from the time you acquired the crypto.
