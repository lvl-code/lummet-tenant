No, it is not illegal for individual Australian punters. The IGA strongly targets the operators, making it an offense for companies to offer or advertise interactive real-money games to Aussies. As a player, you are not breaking the law by signing up and playing at an offshore site.

Are my casino and pokie winnings taxed by the ATO?

Generally, no. The Australian Taxation Office (ATO) considers gambling to be a recreational activity or a game of chance, rather than a profession or steady income stream.
