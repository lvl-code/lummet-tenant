The French Market: ANJ vs. Offshore Platforms

? Foire Aux Questions (FAQ)

Est-il légal de jouer sur un casino en ligne en France ?

For operators, offering unlicensed casino games on French soil is illegal. However, for individual French citizens, playing on international offshore casinos is not criminalized. Players are free to sign up and wager on foreign sites, though they do so without the direct consumer protection of the ANJ.

Do I need a VPN to play at offshore casinos?

While many crypto casinos and offshore sites accept French registrations directly, certain game studios (like NetEnt) block French IPs. Using a reputable VPN (Virtual Private Network) is standard practice for French players to unlock the full potential of these platforms.

Are my casino winnings taxed in France?

In France, gambling winnings obtained purely by chance (like slots or roulette) by recreational players are generally considered non-taxable (non-imposables). Taxation typically only applies to professional gamblers (like poker players) whose primary source of income is derived from strategic play.
