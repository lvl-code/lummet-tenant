The American iGaming Market: State Regulated vs. Sweepstakes vs. Offshore

Currently, only a handful of states (such as New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Michigan, and West Virginia) have fully legalized and state-regulated real money online casinos. For players outside these states, the market offers two incredibly popular and accessible alternatives: Sweepstakes Casinos and Offshore Crypto Platforms.

For the individual player, there is no federal law that makes it a crime to simply play at an offshore online casino. Sweepstakes casinos technically award "prizes" rather than gambling winnings. Once you have accumulated enough 'Sweeps Coins' (usually won through gameplay), you can submit a redemption request. The casino will then wire real USD to your bank account or send it to your e-wallet/crypto address.
