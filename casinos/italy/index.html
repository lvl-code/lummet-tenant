L'Evoluzione del Gioco: ADM vs. Casinò Non AAMS nel 2026

The Italian iGaming market is fundamentally split into two sectors. First is the regulated local market, governed by ADM (Agenzia delle Dogane e dei Monopoli), previously known as AAMS. These platforms offer absolute legal protection, enforce strict responsible gambling tools, and mandate digital identity verification via SPID or CIE. While highly secure, ADM casinos often feature lower maximum bets, smaller bonuses, and strict deposit caps.

Tassazione delle Vincite (Tax-Free vs Declared)

Understanding taxation is crucial. If you play on an ADM-licensed site, your winnings are automatically taxed at the source. The money you withdraw is 100% yours with no need to declare it to the Agenzia delle Entrate. Conversely, winnings accumulated on Non AAMS / Curacao crypto casinos technically fall under "redditi diversi" (miscellaneous income) and should be declared in your annual Italian tax return if they are cashed out to an Italian bank account.

No, it is not a criminal offense for an individual player to register and play on international platforms. The ADM regulations strictly target the *operators*, prohibiting them from explicitly marketing or advertising within Italy without a local license.

A "Bonus Senza Deposito" is a no-deposit bonus. It's a highly sought-after promotion in the Italian market where casinos award you free spins or a small cash balance (e.g., €10) simply for verifying your account, allowing you to test the platform risk-free.
