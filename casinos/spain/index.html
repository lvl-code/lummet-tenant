El Mercado Español: DGOJ vs. Casinos Internacionales en 2026

Spain features one of the most rigorously regulated iGaming markets in Europe, managed by the DGOJ (Dirección General de Ordenación del Juego). Platforms operating locally must adhere to stringent rules: mandatory identity verification via DNI or NIE within minutes of registration, strict deposit limits (€600/day default), and integration with the RGIAJ (the national self-exclusion registry). Furthermore, Spanish regulations heavily restrict the way welcome bonuses can be advertised to new players.

Tributación de las Ganancias (IRPF & Declaración)

Unlike some European countries with tax-free gambling models, Spain taxes net gambling winnings. Whether you play on a DGOJ site or an international crypto casino, net profits (winnings minus losses) must be declared in your annual Declaración de la Renta (IRPF) as "ganancias patrimoniales" if they exceed the legal threshold (usually €1,000 combined with other income). Players offshore are responsible for tracking and reporting these earnings independently.
