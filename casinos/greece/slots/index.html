Taxation on Slot Winnings in Greece

Greek law is unique regarding how it taxes slot players. The tax is calculated per "gaming session." A session is defined as the time from when you log in to when you log out.

  • Under €100: Tax-free.
  • €100.01 - €500: 15% tax on the amount exceeding €100.
  • Over €500: 20% tax on the amount exceeding €500.

On EEEP-licensed sites, this is automatically deducted. On international crypto sites, you receive the full amount, and it is your responsibility to declare it to the Independent Authority for Public Revenue (AADE).
