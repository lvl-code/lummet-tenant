The Greek Market: EEEP (HGC) vs. Offshore

Greece has a fully regulated iGaming environment overseen by the Hellenic Gaming Commission (EEEP - Επιτροπή Εποπτείας και Ελέγχου Παιγνίων). To play on a locally licensed platform, players must be at least 21 years old and verify their identity via KYC procedures linked to local registries.

Why Greek Players Seek International "Offshore" Sites

While the local market provides safety, it comes with strict limitations designed for extreme harm reduction. On EEEP-licensed casinos, slot machines have a strict €2 maximum bet limit and a mandatory 3-second minimum spin duration. For high-rollers and fast-paced players, this is incredibly restrictive. This has led to a massive surge in Greek players utilizing offshore and crypto casinos (Curacao, MGA), which completely bypass these gameplay restrictions, offering unrestricted betting, instant spin features, and anonymous gameplay without tying into local databases.

Taxation on Winnings (Φόρος στα Κέρδη)

Understanding taxation is vital. If you play at an EEEP-licensed casino, your winnings are taxed on a per-session basis and withheld automatically by the operator. The brackets are: 0% tax up to €100, 15% tax for €100.01 to €500, and 20% for any winnings over €500. By playing at international offshore casinos, operators do not automatically withhold these taxes, leaving the player with 100% of their immediate payout, though local tax reporting obligations ultimately fall upon the individual.

In Greece, you must be strictly 21 years old to register and gamble at EEEP-licensed local sites. While some international platforms accept players at 18+, we strongly advise adhering to your local age restrictions.
