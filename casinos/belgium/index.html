Belgium iGaming: Legalities, Bancontact & Limits

The Belgian Gaming Commission (BGC) manages one of Europe's most structured markets. Only operators with a physical presence (land-based partners) can obtain A+ or B+ licenses. While this ensures massive security, it has introduced the €200 per week deposit limit, which applies across all licensed platforms to prevent excessive gambling.

Bancontact: The Belgian Heart of Banking

For players in Brussels, Antwerp, or Liège, Bancontact remains the king of payments. Most reputable Belgian casinos offer Bancontact via payment gateways like HiPay. Deposits are instant, while withdrawals are usually processed via SEPA bank transfer, typically arriving within 24–48 hours.

A+ Licenses vs. B+ Arcade Licenses

Understanding the difference is key: A+ Licenses allow full video slots and table games (like Evolution Live Roulette), whereas B+ Licenses are restricted to 'Dice Games' and 'Dice Slots.' If you are looking for authentic Vegas-style slots, ensure your chosen site holds an A+ designation or a reputable international equivalent like MGA (Malta).

? Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How can I increase my deposit limit in Belgium?

On BGC-licensed sites, the €200 limit is standard. You can request a limit increase through your account settings. The operator then checks with the National Bank to ensure you are not listed as having payment arrears; if you pass, the limit is lifted after 72 hours.

Can I play if I am on the EPIS list?

The EPIS system is a database of excluded persons. If you are on this list, you cannot play at BGC-licensed sites. Many players look toward international (MGA/Curacao) casinos to bypass these local registry restrictions, though we always urge responsible play.

What are 'Dice Games' in Belgium?

Dice games are a Belgian specialty, designed to comply with B+ licenses. They combine elements of classic slots with strategy, requiring players to place dice columns to form winning lines. They are incredibly popular and offer some of the highest RTPs in the market.
